Our approach

A little more care. A little less to think about.

Considered cleaning starts with understanding the space. For homes and workspaces, that means talking about what matters before deciding what the clean should involve.

The starting point

Start with the space, not a standard script.

The kitchen where meals come together. A shared workspace used all day. A detail that keeps slipping down the list.

Each gives a different starting point. Explain how your space is used, what you notice and what you expect from a clean. That context helps make the conversation specific, rather than relying on a standard checklist.

Care in practice

Three ideas that guide the conversation.

Attentive

Notice what needs attention.

A kitchen worktop may be the first thing you mention; an overlooked edge or corner may matter just as much. Point out the details you don’t want lost in the bigger picture.

Clear

Make room for the questions.

Talk about the rooms involved, the focus of the clean and anything you are unsure about. A shared understanding of scope matters more than assumptions about what is included.

Considered

Let the space guide the conversation.

A home and a shared workspace have different rhythms. Explain how the rooms are used, the surfaces involved and where care is most needed, so the discussion stays relevant to you.

From enquiry to agreement

A clear conversation, from the start.

01

Share your space and priorities

Tell us whether it’s a home or workspace, the areas involved and what you would most like to focus on.

02

Discuss the focus and scope

Talk through the kind of clean you have in mind, the details that need attention and any questions about the space.

03

Agree practical next steps

Once the scope is understood, discuss the quote and practical arrangements before confirming a booking.

An enquiry opens the conversation. It does not confirm a booking; scope and practical arrangements need to be agreed first.
